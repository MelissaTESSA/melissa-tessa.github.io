About Me

I am a PhD student in computer science at the Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT), University of Luxembourg, where I work on enabling large language models to generate secure code. My research looks at the robustness of secure code generation defenses under adversarial prompts, package hallucination in code LLMs, and machine unlearning.

I graduated as a State Engineer in computer science from the Higher National School of Computer Science (ESI) in Algiers in 2023.

I hold a Master 2 in Big Data and Data Mining from Paris 8 University, obtained in 2024.
